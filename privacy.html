Privacy Policy - Mantec

Last updated: July 2026

Mantec respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

Information Collection

Mantec does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. The app does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location data, or device information.

User Content

Text entered by users for analysis and the analysis history are stored only locally on the user's device. This information is not uploaded, sold, or shared with third parties.

Data Security

Since Mantec does not collect personal data, there is no personal information stored on external servers.

Children's Privacy

Mantec does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.
